Useful answers

Frequently asked questions

How should veterinary emergency care be shown?

Only with confirmed numbers and genuine duty hours. We will not claim 24/7 service without evidence.

Will the website work on mobile and be search-friendly?

Yes. We plan responsive layouts, semantic headings, meta titles, descriptive links, optimised images and the technical SEO basics. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Can I update the copy, photos and offers later?

Yes. We can agree on simple maintenance or an appropriate CMS, depending on how often your information changes.
