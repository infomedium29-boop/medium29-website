Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Policy explains how the website medium29.com uses cookies, browser local storage and similar technologies, and how you can manage your choices.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or its service providers may save in your browser. Similar technologies, such as localStorage storage, can also remember settings in your browser.

2. Technologies used by this website

Name / technologyTypePurposeDuration / criteria
medium29_cookie_consent_v1Essential browser storageRemembers your cookie category choices so the banner does not appear on every visit.Up to 180 days unless you clear browser data or change your preferences sooner.
hCaptcha on the contact formSecurity technologyProtects the contact form against bots, spam and misuse. The provider may process technical data and use security technologies needed for verification.According to hCaptcha's policies and technical requirements.
Cloudflare / network securityEssential infrastructureContent delivery, network protection and website availability. Depending on security events, Cloudflare may use essential technical identifiers or cookies.As technically required and according to the provider's policies.

3. Analytics and marketing cookies

This version of the website does not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other analytics or marketing tools before consent. If such tools are added later, they must be connected to the consent system so optional categories remain off until visitors actively accept them.

5. How to accept, reject or withdraw consent

On your first visit, you can accept all optional categories, reject them or view detailed settings. Closing the banner without accepting means the website continues with essential technologies only.

You can change your choice at any time by clicking: open cookie settings.

6. Clearing browser data

You can delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. After clearing them, the website may ask you to choose again.

7. Third-party providers

For information about data processing by Web3Forms, hCaptcha, Cloudflare and other technical providers, please see our Privacy Policy.

Note: If analytics, advertising, embedded video, newsletters, chat widgets or other third-party services are added, this Policy and the consent setup must be updated.
